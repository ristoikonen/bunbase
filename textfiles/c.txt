AI model weights are the foundational numeric parameters learned during training, acting as the adjustable synaptic connections within a neural network. 

When raw data—such as text tokens or image pixels—flows through the network's layers, it is multiplied and adjusted by these weights to determine feature importance. 

Ultimately, a model's weights represent its distilled long-term memory and capability, transforming billions of training examples into a compact mathematical recipe capable of generating real-time predictions, classifications, and complex contextual reasoning.
